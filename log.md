## [2026-08-25] query | reconciling the 3x acceleration ceiling with two-year irradiations
updated: questions/How are fuel compacts irradiated in qualification testing (new section), concepts/Fuel Qualification Irradiation Testing (conflict callout)
notes: Reader spotted that 1095 days of service exposure at 3x acceleration is one year, not the two years the chapter quotes for full burnup — implying an effective factor near 1.5x. Source never reconciles the two figures. Four candidate explanations recorded, strongest being (1) different endpoints, since the 1095-day figure is design SERVICE burnup while the two-year figure is FULL burnup under a high-burnup goal, and test burnups in the wiki run 14-29% FIMA against service below 11% — a ~2x higher target closes the arithmetic exactly at 3x; and (2) "less than three" is an inequality. Also recorded: calendar vs at-power time (outages confirmed on p.69) and the decoupling of burnup from fast fluence acceleration. General lesson flagged: "acceleration factor" is underspecified unless its basis is stated, which also bears on the confounded German/US R/B comparison.
